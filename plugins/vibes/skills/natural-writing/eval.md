# Natural writing evaluation

Mark every item pass or fail. Revise every failure before returning or approving a draft.

1. **Material fidelity:** Are the facts, commitments, boundaries, requests, numbers, tense, uncertainty, perspective, and intended tone preserved without treating sentence count or every rationale clause as content?
2. **No invention:** Did the revision avoid adding unsupported context, certainty, motives, warmth, shared history, or consequences?
3. **Attached qualifications:** Does every qualification or contrast stay with the fact it changes?
4. **Attached outcomes:** Do concrete conditions and alternative causes flow directly into their shared outcome or decision in the same sentence?
5. **Policy test:** When a standing rule merely mediates an outcome, is its meaning carried by the alternative avoided rather than a detached rationale sentence? If the rule itself is the news, was it correctly kept separate?
6. **Real thought boundaries:** Do new sentences mark genuine changes in subject, event, decision, or next step, with no report-like fact stacking or mechanical transitions?
7. **Literal language:** Were metaphors and flourishes removed when they replace a clearer literal statement, while figurative language that clarifies, carries the writer's voice, or serves the requested style was preserved?
8. **Natural language:** Is the wording plain, specific, concise, and appropriate to the register, without filler, puffery, repetition, jargon, synonym cycling, robotic symmetry, or artificial warmth?
9. **Restraint and delivery:** Was already-natural prose left largely alone, and does the response provide the complete requested draft plus a short **What changed** section in a fenced `text` block when copy-ready text was requested?
